# Design — TrueStride Equine

## Direction
Luxury through restraint. Simple, elegant, editorial, spacious, horse-first. No clutter, generic corporate cards, loud effects, or dealership aesthetics.

## Colour ratio — non-negotiable
Overall site-wide visual balance: **50% silver, 30% white, 20% black**. This is an overall target, not a rigid ratio per section.
- Silver `#C5C8CC`
- Light silver `#E5E7E9`
- Mid silver `#AEB3B8`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Soft white `#F7F7F5`
- Charcoal `#202124`
- Black `#111214`
- Muted text `#777B80`

Silver is the dominant surface/neutral. White provides breathing room. Black/charcoal provides typography, contrast, and occasional dark sections. Avoid gold, bright accents, loud gradients, glossy effects, and metallic textures.

## Typography
- Display/headings: **Cormorant Garamond**, elegant editorial serif.
- Body/UI: **Inter**, clean sans-serif.
- Use `next/font` where possible; include fallbacks.
- Large expressive headlines, short paragraphs, generous line-height.
- Uppercase only for small eyebrow labels with restrained tracking.
- No decorative fonts, heavy bold headings, or tiny mobile text.

## Layout
Generous whitespace, consistent max-width container and spacing scale, editorial split layouts, large imagery, fine dividers, restrained labels. Use asymmetry sparingly. Avoid repetitive rounded cards, heavy shadows, borders around everything, and SaaS-dashboard patterns.

## Photography
Use authentic premium editorial photography of a **bay thoroughbred**: rich reddish-brown coat, black mane/tail and dark points. Natural light, realistic anatomy, nuanced shadows, cinematic framing. Show details, preparation, travel, and arrival.
Vehicle: refined silver/grey purpose-built horse transport vehicle. **The initial hero is horse-first; vehicle is introduced later on scroll.**
Never use cargo trucks, livestock transport imagery, unrelated luxury cars, cartoon horses, or artificial-looking horses. Do not cover the horse’s face with text. Use overlays only for legibility.

## Navigation
Transparent over hero, transitioning smoothly to a solid white/soft-white header after scrolling. Minimal wordmark, concise links, one restrained CTA. Accessible mobile menu. No abrupt layout shift.

## Feel and avoid
Quiet confidence, precision, calm movement, emotional depth, premium craft. Avoid gold clichés, neon, oversized icons, noisy gradients, dramatic parallax, autoplay sound, long animations, generic stock imagery, and clutter.
