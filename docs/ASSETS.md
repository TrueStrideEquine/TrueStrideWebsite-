# Assets and Fonts

## Folders
`public/images/brand/`, `horses/`, `vehicles/`, `lifestyle/`, `public/icons/`, `public/fonts/`.

## Art direction
Authentic premium editorial photography. Preferred horse: bay thoroughbred with reddish-brown coat and black mane/tail/dark points. Vehicle: refined silver/grey purpose-built horse transport vehicle.

## Rules
Do not invent/download assets without approval or use unlicensed copyrighted imagery. Do not create fake logos. Use descriptive lowercase hyphenated filenames. Keep assets organised, use Next.js Image where appropriate, provide alt text and focal positioning, reserve dimensions/aspect ratios.

## Fonts
Cormorant Garamond for display, Inter for body/UI, subject to availability/licensing. Prefer `next/font`; load only required weights/styles and provide fallbacks.
