# Project Brief

**Brand:** TrueStride Equine, a premium horse-first equine transportation brand.

**Purpose:** Communicate care for the horse, thoughtful engineering, and a considered journey from preparation to arrival.

**Audience:** Discerning horse owners and equestrian professionals.

**Brand attributes:** Horse-first, luxurious, restrained, elegant, calm, confident, precise, modern, editorial, trustworthy.

**Core narrative:** The horse is the emotional centre. The vehicle supports the horse’s journey and is revealed later—not as the first-screen hero.

**Guiding line:** Horse first. Thoughtful engineering. A more considered journey.

**Success:** Visitors understand the philosophy, discover TS-01, follow the journey narrative, and can enquire. The site must be fast, accessible, responsive, and consistent.
