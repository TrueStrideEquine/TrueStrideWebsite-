# Site Map and Sections

## Single-page structure

All primary content lives on `/` in one continuous scrolling experience. The old section paths redirect to their matching fragment on the homepage.

## Global navigation

Our World `#our-world` · TS-01 `#ts-01` · The Journey `#journey` · Philosophy `#philosophy` · Contact `#contact` · CTA “Begin a Conversation” (`#contact`).

## Homepage `/`

1. **Hero:** bay thoroughbred; “Every Journey Begins With Care.” Supporting copy: “Thoughtfully engineered transport. A calmer, more considered journey for the horse at its heart.” CTAs: “Enter Our World” (smooth-scroll to next section), “Discover TS-01”. Vehicle must not dominate initial view.
2. **Our World:** “More Than Transportation. A Better Journey.” Horse image and concise brand introduction, followed by four pillars: Horse First, Thoughtful Engineering, Considered Travel, and Refined Craftsmanship. The horse and engineering reveal also sits within this section.
3. **TS-01:** “Purposeful by Design.” Introduce engineering and vehicle imagery after the horse-first opening, with 3–4 high-level feature themes and an “Explore TS-01” CTA that targets `#ts-01`.
4. **The Journey:** Prepare, Travel, Arrive in a connected editorial sequence (`#journey`).
5. **Philosophy:** “Care Is in Every Detail.” Horse close-up and concise point of view (`#philosophy`).
6. **Contact:** “Let’s Begin a Conversation.” Enquiry form and CTA (`#contact`).
7. **Footer:** wordmark, section links, and labelled legal placeholders until verified details are available.

## Legacy section URLs

`/our-world` → `/#our-world`; `/ts-01` → `/#ts-01`; `/journey` → `/#journey`; `/philosophy` → `/#philosophy`; `/contact` → `/#contact`.

These paths are compatibility redirects, not separate page destinations.

## Content guardrail

Never invent price, dimensions, capacity, materials, certifications, safety claims, technical specs, testimonials, addresses, phone numbers, emails, or social accounts. Use clearly marked placeholders until verified.
